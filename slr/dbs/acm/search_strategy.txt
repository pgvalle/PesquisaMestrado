ACM DIGITAL LIBRARY SEARCH STRATEGY
===================================

Database:
ACM Digital Library

Goal:
Approximate a title + abstract + keywords search.

Field limitation:
-----------------
ACM does not provide a single direct equivalent of Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY in the same simple form. Title, Abstract, and Keyword are exposed separately.

Preferred conceptual formulation:
---------------------------------
For each concept group, search:
Title OR Abstract OR Keyword

Then combine the two concept groups with AND.

Practical interface note:
-------------------------
ACM's graphical Advanced Search interface can make cross-field OR combinations awkward because separate Search Within rows are commonly combined with AND.

Fallback:
---------
If the field-specific construction is impractical in the interface, use the Anywhere field with:

("reactive programming" OR "reactive languages" OR "synchronous programming" OR "synchronous languages" OR "structured concurrency" OR "functional reactive programming" OR FRP)
AND
("embedded system" OR "embedded systems" OR "embedded device" OR "embedded devices" OR microcontroller OR microcontrollers OR "single-board computer" OR "single-board computers" OR Arduino OR ESP32 OR "Raspberry Pi" OR "resource-constrained")

Important fallback limitation:
------------------------------
Anywhere is broader than a title/abstract/keywords-only search and may match terms occurring elsewhere in the document/full text.

Methodological note:
--------------------
If the fallback is used, document that ACM was searched using a broader field scope than Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY.
